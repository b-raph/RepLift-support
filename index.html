In English

What RepLift does

You log your sets while you train, not afterwards. The app remembers what you lifted last time and suggests the next step.

On your wristiPhone + Apple Watch

Workouts start on iPhone and arrive on Apple Watch. Log your sets without reaching for your phone; the rest timer starts on its own. The Android version has no watch app.

120 illustrated exercises

The same catalogue on both, sorted by muscle group and equipment. When a machine is taken, RepLift offers a substitute that trains the same thing with whatever is free. Twelve programmes ship on iPhone, nineteen on Android.

Your data stays yours

No account, no server, no ads, no trackers. RepLift works entirely offline, and you can export your whole history as JSON and CSV whenever you like.
